4 customers. Fast service tips better.

How to play

  1. At the spit, shave meat into the tray. Sweep up and down so you keep cutting the crispy outer crust. Cutting one spot too long gives pale meat.
  2. At the board, fill the bread with meat from the tray, then the vegetables and sauces on the ticket.
  3. Serve when every dot on the ticket is green. Red means too much, or something the customer said no to.
  4. Tips depend on the meat weight, the crust, the toppings, spills and speed. Spend them on upgrades between days.
